# Excellence Competition on Frontier Financial Research — Competition Guidelines

**"Dual Excellence in Finance: Who Will Take the Lead?" — Frontier Financial Research Competition**

**Co-organizers:** Taiwan Economic Association; Institute of Economics, Academia Sinica; Taiwan Academy of Banking and Finance (TABF)
**Strategic Partners:** Bank SinoPac; Joint Credit Information Center (JCIC)
**Topic Partner:** Mega International Commercial Bank
**Co-host:** Taiwan Econometric Society

---

## 1. Purpose

This competition centers on **rigorous quantitative research × frontier financial issues × presentation skills**. Participating teams are encouraged to produce research grounded in data, with methodological awareness or industry implications.

Through the presentation of results and exchanges with partner institutions, the competition aims to help students strengthen their research and practical application skills, explore career paths in finance, and build a body of research on frontier issues for the partner institutions. It promotes industry–academia collaboration and talent matching, jointly advancing the integration of financial practice, academic economic research methods, and the cultivation of young talent.

---

## 2. Eligibility and Team Composition

1. Open to third-year and fourth-year undergraduate students, master's students (excluding in-service/executive programs), and doctoral students in economics, business and management, or other related fields at universities and colleges in Taiwan.
2. Each team should, in principle, have 3–5 members, formed by the students themselves. Teams may include members from different departments and degree levels, but all members must be currently enrolled students at the same university. Members may not join more than one team.
3. Each team must have one current university faculty member as its advisor, who provides academic consultation and advice. The advisor may not carry out the research design, data analysis, presentation preparation, or defense preparation on the team's behalf.

---

## 3. Research Themes

Each team must select one research theme at registration. During the research period, teams may use the suggested research topics listed below or define their own topic within the chosen theme.

Research themes are provided by the strategic and topic partners:

### (1) Theme: Generative AI and Behavioral Finance Research (provided by Bank SinoPac)

**Suggested research topics:**

- Constructing Taiwanese investor segments with cumulative memory, and validating their usefulness for testing behavioral finance theories and predicting client behavior
- Reproduction and stability analysis of investment behavioral biases (e.g., loss aversion, the disposition effect) in generative AI agents
- Differences in AI-simulated decisions and parameter sensitivity analysis across Taiwanese investor segments with different profiles under dynamic market conditions
- The potential and practical limitations of using GenAI agents with cumulative memory to predict financial clients' investment behavior

### (2) Theme: Credit Risk Management in Financial Markets (provided by the Joint Credit Information Center)

**Suggested research topics:**

- The impact of ESG and climate change on credit risk and financial resilience
- Credit building and risk management under financial inclusion
- Construction and application of a financial market credit risk index

**Available data:**

1. Data from the OpenData section of the JCIC official website
   (https://www.jcic.org.tw/main_ch/download_page.aspx?uid=213&pid=190)
2. Statistics on default rates and credit scores for corporate finance, overall and by industry (e.g., wholesale, retail, services).
3. Statistics on default rates and credit scores for consumer finance, overall and by business line (e.g., credit cards, mortgages, personal loans).

### (3) Theme: Digital Finance, Stablecoins, and Cross-Border Payments (provided by Mega International Commercial Bank)

**Suggested research topics:**

- The impact of stablecoins on the efficiency and cost of cross-border payments and on financial supervision
- Competition between digital currencies and banks' traditional fiat currency services
- The impact of cross-border payment innovation on SME financing and trade
- The potential impact of stablecoin development on monetary policy transmission and capital flows
- Strategic assessment of Taiwanese financial institutions entering digital asset infrastructure

**Research data may draw on the following public sources:**

1. Official macroeconomic and financial statistics (money, deposits, capital flows, cross-border payments): IMF, BIS, ECB, Fed, Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), World Bank
2. On-chain and virtual asset market data (stablecoins, trading volume, RWA, chain-level activity): Coin Metrics, DefiLlama, Visa/Allium, RWA.xyz
3. Trade and corporate financing data (SME financing, trade finance gaps, imports and exports): ADB, OECD, ICC, UN Comtrade, WTO
4. Industry surveys and market research (adoption rates, budgets, demand, strategy): Fireblocks, EY, BCG, PwC, KPMG, Chainalysis

## 4. Competition Timeline

### (1) Registration

From **Thursday, October 15, 2026, 09:00** to **Wednesday, November 18, 2026, 23:59**.

During the registration period, the team captain should log in to the competition website https://web.tabf.org.tw/frontierfin, fill in the registration information on behalf of the team, and select a research theme to complete the registration entry.

After the entry is submitted, team members and the advisor will receive a notification. They must complete their personal information as instructed in the notification, including the consents related to participation and the collection of personal data, to complete the registration process. Teams that do not complete the entry and the members' personal information will be treated as not registered.

An online information session is planned for early November to explain the competition rules and research themes. See the latest news on the competition website https://web.tabf.org.tw/frontierfin. Participation is strongly encouraged.

### (2) Online Consultation Meetings by Research Theme

Within two weeks after registration closes, online consultation meetings will be held separately for each research theme. Each team should attend the meeting for its selected theme. The strategic and topic partners will attend, and teams may consult them on research directions and expected outcomes before beginning their research. Meeting times will be announced in the latest news on the competition website https://web.tabf.org.tw/frontierfin.

### (3) Research Submission

Research results must be uploaded between **Tuesday, December 1, 2026** and **Monday, December 21, 2026, 23:59**, in PDF format. Teams that do not upload within this period will be deemed to have withdrawn.

The submission is positioned as a **"research proposal with preliminary empirical analysis."** A complete, publication-ready paper is not required within the limited time. However, the research question should be clear, data processing transparent, methodological choices reasonable, and preliminary results credible, and the submission should offer conclusions, recommendations, or applications of reference value.

1. **Written research report** (PDF format)
   In principle 8–12 pages, excluding appendices and references. It must include a cover page, introduction, literature review, research methodology, data description, empirical results, conclusion, and references. See the written report template for details.
2. **AI use statement:** describe the names of the tools used, the stages in which they were used, and their purposes.
3. **Replication package** (cloud link with download permission)
   Teams are encouraged to provide a replication package as supplementary review material (optional).

### (4) Stage 1: Written Review

For each research theme, the judges will in principle select 5 teams for the **Finals Group** and 5 teams for the **Presentation Group** to advance to that theme's finals. However, the organizers may reduce the number of selected teams and awards if there are not enough qualified teams, depending on the quality of the submitted reports.

Stage 1 results are scheduled to be announced on the competition website by group ("Finals Group" and "Presentation Group") on **Tuesday, January 12, 2027, at 2:00 p.m.**, and teams will be notified by email.

### (5) Stage 2: Finals

1. **Documents required before the finals**
   Teams in the "Finals Group" and "Presentation Group" must upload their **research presentation slides** by **Tuesday, January 19, 2027, 23:59**. Slides should in principle be no more than 15 pages, excluding appendices, in PDF or PPT format.
2. The "Finals Group" and "Presentation Group" will present in person at the finals, scheduled for **Friday, January 22, 2027, 09:00–17:00**, at TABF (No. 62, Sec. 3, Roosevelt Rd., Taipei City). See the latest news on the competition website https://web.tabf.org.tw/frontierfin for the event schedule. In principle, all team members should attend.

### (6) Evaluation Criteria

#### 1. Stage 1: Written Review

| Criterion                                          | Weight | Description                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Research question and data acquisition             | 25%    | The question is clear, important, and relevant to financial industry policy; data sources are clearly stated, and data acquisition and processing are appropriate.    |
| Identification strategy and defense of assumptions | 30%    | The method suits the research question, and the team can argue that the identifying assumptions hold in the research context.                                         |
| Interpretation of results and policy linkage       | 25%    | Results and limitations are reported honestly; recommendations are specific and supported by research evidence.                                                       |
| Creativity of the question or method               | 20%    | The research and analysis process is clearly explained; the research question or method is creative and helps clarify the problem or achieve the research objective.* |

*Note: Scored based on the explanations in the submitted materials. Teams are encouraged to provide a replication package as supplementary review material (optional).

#### 2. Stage 2: Finals

| Criterion                        | Weight | Description                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Theme and strategy communication | 50%    | Clearly explains the background and core of the research; the strategy is logical, feasible, and consistent with the analysis and objectives. |
| On-site presentation and defense | 35%    | The presentation is complete and well organized, and the team can respond to questions with data or logic.                                    |
| Teamwork and time management     | 15%    | Demonstrates collaboration and presents completely within the allotted time.                                                                  |

## 6. Career Exploration Activities

Teams advancing to Stage 2 will take part in face-to-face career exploration activities with the strategic or topic partners, and will complete a multi-dimensional aptitude assessment to better understand their occupational traits and suitable job categories.

### (1) Multi-Dimensional Aptitude Assessment

Members of "Finals Group" and "Presentation Group" teams will receive an email notification about the aptitude assessment. Please complete the assessment as instructed by **Tuesday, January 19, 2027, 23:59**.

The assessment report can help participants understand their personality traits, strengths, and suitable job categories. With the participant's consent, the data may be included in TABF's financial talent pool as a reference for future talent recommendations and matching, increasing the chance of receiving interview invitations from financial institutions.

### (2) Career Exploration Exchange

1. Partner institutions will share their organizational characteristics, business development, job content, and talent needs, helping participants understand financial industry practice and career development paths and prepare early for job searching.
2. On the day of the finals, participants can meet partner institution representatives in person to present their research results, personal strengths, and career interests, building mutual understanding and exploring future internship, employment, or talent development opportunities. Such opportunities and selection methods depend on each partner institution's actual needs and plans; admission decisions are made by the partner institutions.
